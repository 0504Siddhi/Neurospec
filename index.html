Clinical Context

Limitations & Clinical Boundaries

Not validated on Indian populations or low-cost dry sensors. Single dataset, no external validation. Pre-screening support only, not a diagnosis. Targets for sensitivity reflect programmatic screening trade-offs, not clinical diagnostics. EEG's added value over zero-cost cognitive paper tests (MMSE, Mini-Cog, GPCOG) is not proven here.
  • Dataset provenance: OpenNeuro dataset ds004504 (License: CC0). Recorded at the 2nd Department of Neurology, AHEPA General Hospital of Thessaloniki, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, Thessaloniki, Greece. 88 participants total: 36 Alzheimer's disease (AD), 23 Frontotemporal dementia (FTD), and 29 Healthy controls (CN). Clinical recordings utilized a Nihon Kohden EEG 2100 clinical device with 19 scalp electrodes (10-20 international system) and wet conductive paste (skin impedance < 5 kΩ).
  • Pre-screening only: This tool is engineered solely to assist community screening logistics and hardware budgeting. It does not provide definitive medical confirmation or replace comprehensive clinical workup.
  • Electrode contact impedance: Dataset ds004504 was recorded in hospital conditions with wet electrodes and skin preparation. Field implementations using dry or semi-dry sensors will experience higher contact impedance and movement artifacts.
  • Prevalence is an assumption: Default camp prevalence is set to 7.4% based on the national estimate for adults 60+ in India (Lee et al., 2023, Alzheimer's & Dementia; LASI-DAD). Other studies report different estimates (e.g. 8.44%, Jin et al., 2023). All inputs remain fully editable.
  • Cost scope: Headline unit costs reflect front-end chips and electrodes only (listing prices to be confirmed, date not recorded), excluding PCB fabrication, enclosures, batteries, and leads.
